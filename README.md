# Anomaly: The Final Shape

**Anomaly** is a 2D top-down Ontological Horror Soulslike focused on visceral combat and the psychological weight of survival. Set in the decaying world of **Akasa**, players embody **Kio**, an Elite Hunter of the Black Scythe Organisation, navigating a reality that is physically and conceptually unraveling.

## 🌑 The Lore: The Familiarity of the Unknown

In *Anomaly*, the horror lies in the instability of objects, memories, and the self. 

* **The Three-Souled Hunter:** You play as Kio, a man whose existence is an intersection of three souls. After forty years of service as an Elite Hunter, you are tasked with purging the **Void Hearts** from a world falling into the **Ruination**.
* **A Fragile Reality:** The world of Akasa is undergoing "Structural Rot." Environments and enemies lose their defined shapes, becoming "glitches" in a fractured landscape.
* **The Voice in the Void:** As Kio wanders the silence of the Ruination, a single, deep voice echoes in his mind—a constant companion that offers guidance, though its origin and intent remain shrouded in mystery.
* **The Cost of Power:** To survive, Kio must consume the corruption he hunts. Every "hit" of power increases his efficiency but further distorts his metaphysical connection to the world, pushing him toward an inevitable "overdose" of the soul.

## Gameplay: 

*Anomaly* rejects standard RPG equipment loops and elemental magic. There is only **The Scythe** and the violent metaphysical process known as **The Rend**.

### **The Rend & Soul Weapon Arcs**
After defeating the first Disciple, **Rà'Meska**, Kio gains the power of **The Rend**, a technique that allows him to violently distort the scythe’s blueprint to manifest the properties of defeated entities.

* **Archetype Binding:** Slot different **Soul Weapon Arcs** into your scythe to change its fundamental properties: **Slashing**, **Piercing**, or **Smashing**.
* **Property Manifestation:** The Scythe's identity remains constant, but its physics shift. A **Hammer Arc** makes the Scythe swing with crushing weight, while a **Dagger Arc** allows for rapid-fire strikes.
* **The 130% Efficiency Loop:** While all weapon types remain effective, exploiting a specific weakness (e.g., using a Smashing move on armored foes) grants a **130% damage boost**, rewarding the player with a surge of corrupted power.
* **Arc-Specific Specials:** Each Arc unlocks a unique **Special Move** that often carries a different damage type than the Arc's primary combat flow, allowing for tactical, hybrid playstyles.

### **Kinetic Combat & Visuals**
* **Visual Indicators:** The active Arc is shown through the glowing stones of the Scythe. As corruption deepens, mists bleed from the weapon and Kio's form—visual "Eye Candy" representing his steady descent.
* **Kinetic Weight:** Weight is sold through "Hit-Stop" (momentary pauses on impact), screen shake, and frame-manipulation. The Scythe hits with the "Ghost" mass of its current Soul Arc.
* **The Glitch Aesthetic:** Visual progression is tied to the state of the world—pixelated distortions and frame-skipping effects represent the weapon's unstable nature.
